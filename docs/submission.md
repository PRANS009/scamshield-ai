# Hackathon submission draft

**Name:** ScamShield AI

**Tagline:** Verify Before You Trust.

**Problem:** Students and job seekers must evaluate suspicious links, recruitment messages and internship offers across disconnected sources. ScamShield combines local screening, bounded public research and readable uncertainty in one interface.

**Working core:** React workspace, FastAPI API, controlled research pipeline, SerpApi integration, recruiter-domain comparison, evidence-led reports, QR decoding, local document extraction, PDF export, job discovery, keyword resume matching and user-controlled history/application tracking.

**SerpApi contribution:** The backend plans company/domain queries, calls Google Search, deduplicates evidence leads and records provenance. A conditional third query investigates payment-policy tension. Google Jobs supplies genuine public listings; returned descriptions support sample skill frequencies and advertised compensation display.

**AI contribution:** Optional provider-configurable JSON explanations use report evidence with checked source IDs. Deterministic rules retain control of numeric scores. No model has open-ended tools or authority to declare identity verified.

**Limitations:** Search snippets are not authenticated facts. No guaranteed scam detection, full certificate verification, calibrated salary fairness or automatic monitoring. See feature-status.md for exact coverage.
