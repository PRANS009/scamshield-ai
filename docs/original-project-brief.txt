Problem Statement

Students, fresh graduates, job seekers, and everyday internet users frequently encounter suspicious job offers, fake internships, phishing links, misleading websites, recruiter impersonation, fraudulent messages, and questionable online opportunities.

Existing tools are often fragmented. Users may need separate websites to check a suspicious URL, investigate a company, verify a job posting, compare internship opportunities, or understand potentially harmful contract terms.

Many users also lack the technical knowledge needed to evaluate online risks.

Proposed Solution

Build ScamShield AI, an intelligent web platform that allows users to submit URLs, messages, job postings, internship offers, and relevant documents.

An AI investigation agent uses structured analysis, SerpApi search results, official websites, and available public evidence to identify suspicious indicators and provide understandable recommendations.

The system must distinguish verified facts, suspicious indicators, unverified claims, and missing evidence.

It must never guarantee that a website, recruiter, company, or opportunity is safe.
